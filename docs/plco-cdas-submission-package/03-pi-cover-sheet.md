state in serial plasma

the "Data Access Requestor" and the person who submits the proposal. The

## 3. Attestations

By signing below, the Principal Investigator attests that:

1. They are a permanent employee of the institution named above, at a level
   equivalent to an academic professor (assistant, associate, tenure-track or
   tenured) or a senior researcher, and are not a post-doc, trainee or
   laboratory technician.
2. They are the person responsible for this project: its submission, its
   renewals and its close-out, and for the conduct of the research it
   describes.
3. They have read and agree to the CDAS Data Use Policies, including that the
   data will not be used to identify or contact any participant or physician
   whose information is contained in the data, and that the research proposal
   will be publicly displayed on the CDAS website once access is granted.
4. The research described is legitimate, the data will be used only for the
   purpose described in the proposal, and no attempt will be made to
   re-identify any participant.
5. The individuals listed in section 2 are authorised by the PI to have
   access to the data at this institution, and they have been informed of the
   CDAS Data Use Policies.
6. The analysis is pre-specified as described in the proposal narrative, and
   no parameter will be re-tuned after the data are opened.
7. The information given in this proposal is accurate to the best of the
   PI's knowledge.
