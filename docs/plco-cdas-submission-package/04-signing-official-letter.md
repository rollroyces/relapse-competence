## 1. Purpose

The Cancer Data Access System has approved, or is considering, an
application by the undersigned institution for access to study data from the
Prostate, Lung, Colorectal, and Ovarian Cancer Screening Trial (PLCO). Under
the CDAS data access process, the Signing Official at the institution reviews
the request, the renewals and the close-out of the project, and executes the
Data Transfer and Use Agreement through DocuSign. This letter is the
institutional endorsement that sits behind that step.

employee at a level equivalent to an academic professor, or a senior
researcher, as required by the CDAS data access instructions.

## 3. Institutional commitments

The institution confirms that it will:

- ensure that the data accessed under this project are used only for the
  research described in the proposal, and for no other purpose;
- ensure that no participant or physician whose information appears in the
  data is identified or contacted, and that no re-identification is
  attempted;
- provide the safeguards required by the Data Transfer and Use Agreement,
  including that the data are held in an access-controlled institutional
  environment and that access is limited to the individuals listed above;
- notify the CDAS of any change in the individuals listed above, and of any
  change in the institution's authorised contacts, so that the project's
  permissions and renewals can be kept accurate;
- complete the renewal or the close-out of this project within CDAS's stated
  access term, and not continue to hold the data beyond it.

## 4. Declarations

The institution confirms that it has no interest in the outcome of the
research proposed beyond the scientific and operational interest in the
project, and that the work will not be sponsored by any commercial party
whose products are assessed by it.
