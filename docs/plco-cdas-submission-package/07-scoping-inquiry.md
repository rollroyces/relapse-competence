# Scoping inquiry to CDAS (subject: PLCO)

**Do this first.** It needs no CDAS account, no proposal and no signature, and
it is on the critical path: the answer determines whether the EEMS
biospecimen application — a four-to-six-month route to the plasma aliquots —
and the assay budget are worth pursuing at all.

**Route:** the CDAS contact form, `https://cdas.cancer.gov/contact/`, with
the subject set to **PLCO**. CDAS state that messages are forwarded to the
party best able to answer them. If the PI already has a CDAS account, a
question about an existing project belongs on that project's Discussion tab
instead — but this question precedes any project, so the contact form is
right.

**What is being asked**, in one sentence: how many PLCO participants
diagnosed with lung cancer have both serial plasma at screening visits and an
archived primary tumour?

---

## Draft message

**Subject:** PLCO — scope inquiry before a data-only project proposal

> Dear CDAS team,
>
> I am preparing a data-only project proposal for PLCO and would like to
> confirm the availability of one specific subset before submitting, so that
> the proposal requests only what exists.
>
> The project would test whether a bulk transcript measurement of two ion
> channels (KCNN4 and PIEZO1) in serial plasma separates PLCO participants
> who are diagnosed with lung cancer from matched participants who are not.
> The analysis is pre-specified and the code is public. I would request
> serial plasma at screening visits, matched participants, and the clinical
> data to align the draws with diagnosis, treatment and mortality.
>
> Before submitting, could you tell me, for PLCO participants diagnosed with
> lung cancer:
>
> 1. How many have plasma available from more than one screening visit, and
>    how many have plasma from a single visit only?
> 2. How many of those have primary tumour tissue archived and available
>    with the study data?
> 3. How many have a diagnosis date available for release in a Limited Data
>    Set, and are matched participants who were not diagnosed with lung
>    cancer available on the same basis?
> 4. Is a tumour-tissue request for PLCO lung cancer made through the EEMS
>    preliminary application, and are the deadlines published for the
>    coming period?
>
> A count of participants rather than a file listing would be enough for me
> to size the request and the assays.
>
> Thank you for your help.
>
> [PI] — [PI], [PI]
> [PI] — institutional email

## Notes for the PI

- **The counts, not the data.** Asking for a count first is what keeps the
  proposal within what exists. A proposal that asks for data that turn out to
  be unavailable is what draws a request for additional information.
- **The four questions are in order of importance.** If only one can be
  answered, question 2 is the one that decides whether the tissue request is
  worth making at all.
- **Question 4 is a deadline question**, and the EEMS preliminary
  application has one. The CDAS home page announces the closing dates for
  biospecimen applications; the data-only route is not among them.
- **This is not the proposal.** It asks a scope question, commits to nothing,
  and does not need the Signing Official. The proposal in
  `01-project-details.md` and `02-proposal-narrative.md` is a separate
  document, and it goes through the route in `06-submission-runbook.md`.
