## Why the AGPL matters here

The AGPL's distinctive obligation is the **network use clause (Section 13)**:
if you modify this software and then let other users interact with it
remotely — for example by hosting a modified version of the analysis service —
you must offer those users the corresponding source of your modified version.

For a research package whose whole value proposition is "the provenance of
every number in the results file", that is a feature rather than a
restriction. A commercial licence removes the obligation for anyone who
cannot or does not wish to publish their modifications.
